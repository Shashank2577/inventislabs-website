01 The Gap

India lies in one of the world's most seismically active zones. Over 59% of the landmass is exposed to moderate-to-severe earthquakes.

Seismic zones IV and V cover the Himalayan belt, the Gujarat margin, the Northeast and the Narmada–Son lineament — dense population centres, ports, refineries, nuclear installations and hospitals, all sitting on active fault systems.

An earthquake cannot be stopped. But the interval between the fault rupturing and the ground moving can be measured — and that interval is enough time to stop a gas line, halt an elevator, take a hospital off mains power, and get a hundred thousand people under a table.

  • Automated shutdown

    Critical machinery, gas lines and nuclear facilities halt on command.

  • Mass public alert

    Sirens, mobile apps, SMS and radio broadcast simultaneously.

  • Drop, Cover, Hold On

    Coordinated human response while the building is still standing.

Every second counts in saving lives and protecting infrastructure.

    S-waves arrive — population already prepared

    The shaking hits an organised, warned population rather than an unwarned one.

03 The Node

One box on a pole. Everything after that is arithmetic.

A sealed can, a triaxial sensor, a solar lid and a radio. It has no moving parts and no operator. It watches the ground continuously and speaks only when the ground says something.

  • 200 HzContinuous acquisition on three axes
  • <8 msEdge inference before the uplink
  • 18 moSolar + Li-ion autonomy
  • IP67−20 °C to 70 °C, sealed

07 Origin

Born from necessity, not from a pitch deck.

Inventis Labs is not a typical Silicon Valley-style startup. We are a deep-tech company rooted in Uttar Pradesh, driven by the reality of India's seismic vulnerability.

Seeing the gaps in existing disaster management infrastructure, we set out to build something technologically advanced that was also economically viable for mass deployment in Indian cities.

Working closely with research guidance from IIT Roorkee's Earthquake Engineering department, we developed a system that rivals global standards while being 100% indigenous.

Our journey

  1. 2021Concept & early R&D. Validating MEMS sensor feasibility for Indian conditions.
