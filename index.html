01 The Gap

It starts at the source.

India lies in one of the world's most seismically active zones. Over 59% of the landmass is exposed to moderate-to-severe earthquakes — the Himalayan belt, the Gujarat margin, the Northeast, and the Narmada–Son lineament, all dense with cities, ports, refineries and hospitals.

An earthquake cannot be stopped. But the gap between the fault rupturing and the ground moving can be measured — and that gap is enough time to shut a gas line, floor an elevator, take a hospital off mains, and get a hundred thousand people under a table.

  1. Automated shutdownCritical machinery, gas lines and nuclear facilities halt on command.
  2. Mass public alertSirens, mobile apps, SMS and radio broadcast at once.
  3. Drop, Cover, Hold OnCoordinated human response while the building is still standing.

Every second counts in saving lives and protecting infrastructure.

03 The Node

One box on a pole.
Everything after that is arithmetic.

A sealed can, a triaxial sensor, a solar lid and a radio. No moving parts, no operator, nothing to service in a hurry. It watches the ground continuously and speaks only when the ground says something.

07 Origin

Born from necessity,
not from a pitch deck.

Inventis Labs is not a typical startup. We are a deep-tech company rooted in Uttar Pradesh, driven by the reality of India's seismic vulnerability.

Seeing the gaps in existing disaster infrastructure, we set out to build something technologically serious that was also economically viable for mass deployment in Indian cities.

Working with research guidance from IIT Roorkee's Earthquake Engineering department, we built a system that rivals global standards while being entirely indigenous.

  1. 2021Concept and early R&D. Validating MEMS sensor feasibility for Indian conditions.
  2. 2023Field-ready prototypes and lab testing at IIT Roorkee. First successful pilots.
  3. TodayCommercial launch of EQ-Alert, policy engagement, 1,300+ sensor nodes.

Mission

Democratise access to life-saving early warning technology and build a resilient India through indigenous innovation.

Vision

Establish India as a global leader in earthquake early warning and structural monitoring across emerging economies.

  1. Safety firstEvery decision protects lives and critical infrastructure.
